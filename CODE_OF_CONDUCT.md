# Code of Conduct

SceneXP exists to spread a little happiness. Every experience in this project honors a real person or business, and we ask that the community around the project carry that same spirit. Think of yourself as a guest in someone's home, and of everyone else as a fellow guest.

## Our standard

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background, identity, or level of experience. In issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space, we expect you to:

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give feedback about the work, never about the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you criticize.
- Respect the people and businesses the experiences honor. They did not ask to be discussed on the internet.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution, and that a polite no is still a no.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, sexualized language or imagery, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and any conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting. Because our experiences depict real people, mockery or unkind commentary aimed at an honoree is treated exactly like conduct aimed at a contributor.

## Enforcement

If someone's behavior falls short of this standard, please reach out through the [contact page](https://www.scenexp.com/contact.html). Reports are handled privately and with care. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat offenders from project spaces.
