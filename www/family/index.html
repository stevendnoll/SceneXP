Welcome to Shipwreck Cove

Evening! One round of pirate golf, on the house. Take your time out there, the course is all yours tonight. A family is playing their summer round, feel free to say hi.

  • Move: W A S D or the arrow keys.
  • Look: move the mouse. Click the scene once to capture the cursor. Press Esc to release it.
  • Move & look: use the left joystick to walk and the right joystick to look around.
  • The ship: cross the gangplank at the north path and you are aboard. Three holes play on deck.
  • The plank: the famous hole 7. You can walk the plank yourself, the ropes will keep you dry.
  • The scorecard: the board by the entrance lists a few things worth finding tonight.
  • The conch shell: right here on the counter. Hold it to your ear and see.
  • Autopilot: press T or the tour button (top-left) and the course will show you around. Move to take back the controls.
  • Settings: the gear icon (top-right) adjusts movement and look sensitivity.
  • Heading out? The Home button (top-right) takes you back to the main site.
