The Ocean

Ninety seconds at the water's edge, beginning on an ordinary bright afternoon.

Please know before you start that this one is meant to unsettle you. The weather turns, the surf keeps rising, and the sea comes over you at the end. There is lightning, so the screen flashes from time to time. It plays without sound, so you are welcome to watch it anywhere.

You can watch it again as many times as you wish.

The sea has taken the beach.

Thank you for watching. It happens the same way every time.
