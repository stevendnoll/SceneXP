X's and O's

Ten plays, and you are on offense for every one of them. The farther you carry the ball, the more the play is worth.

  1. Call a play. Pick one from the playbook, and choose a defense to face or leave it to chance.
  2. Snap the ball. Take as long as you like reading the formation first.
  3. Throw or run. Hit a receiver, or keep it yourself and see how far you get.

On a keyboard: Space, S or Q snaps the ball, C changes the play before you do, A to D throw to that receiver, and K keeps it.

What a play is worth

  • 5 past the first line
  • 15 past the second
  • 30 past the third
  • 50 all the way across
  • -5 sacked holding the ball
  • -10 intercepted

Fifty a play is the most you can get, so 500 is a perfect game. Good luck.
