3D worlds, games, and experiments, right in your browser

Worlds to walk through, games to play, and experiments that exist to show you what a browser can do. No app, no download, nothing to install.

Worlds and games

  • A garden you plant and tend yourself

    Fractal Garden

    Plant a garden of fractal trees, then watch it live through the years. Choose from seventeen species, each one grown from its own seed so no two are alike, then tend them through spring blossom, summer fruit, autumn color and winter snow. Every tree you plant is still standing when you come back.

    Plant your first tree →

  • A study of water and weather

    High Water

    Ninety seconds at the water's edge, beginning on an ordinary bright afternoon. The sky closes over, lightning starts to work along the cloud base, and the surf keeps rising. Please know before you start that the sea does not stay where it belongs.

    Stand at the shore →

  • Built for the joy of flying through space

    Earth Defense

    Fly a single ship in orbit above a massive Earth while twelve Martian raiders close on the installations below. There is no fire button to learn: line a raider up in the middle of your view and your guns take care of the rest.

    Take the helm →

  • For Benoit Mandelbrot

    The Mandelbrot Set

    The most famous shape in mathematics floats in a quiet starfield. Choose a destination on its burning edge, press play, and the auto zoom dives hundreds of billions of times deep, until the numbers themselves run out.

    Begin the dive →

What you will find here

The collection wanders on purpose, so it is sorted into three groups above. Here is what each of them holds, and every heading below takes you straight to it.

  • Worlds and games

    The ones built for their own sake. Defend Earth from a Martian fleet, dive hundreds of billions of times deep into the Mandelbrot set, watch a modelled ocean shoal and break on a measured beach, or plant a garden of fractal trees and tend it through the seasons.

  • Small business tributes

    Real shops, rebuilt as places you can walk into. A tire shop on historic Railroad Ave with a car up on the lift, and a backyard vegetable garden with raised beds and a greenhouse to step inside.

  • Personal tributes

    Worlds made for a particular person, or to hold on to a particular memory. A quiet trail along the Gunpowder, a karaoke night at the corner bar, a Zumba class, a bug hunt in the jasmine, a round of pirate mini golf.

Every one of them runs in your browser

No app, no download, no account, and nothing to install. Each experience is browser-native HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with Three.js doing the drawing, served from this domain and nowhere else. There are no third-party scripts, no CDNs, and no trackers anywhere on the site. Open a link and you are already inside.

Built for someone in particular

Several of the worlds above began as a gift. Someone knew a person who deserved a world, or a business that deserved to be more than another template website, and told us the story. We still love making those, so if a person or a place came to mind while you were browsing, we would be glad to hear about it.
