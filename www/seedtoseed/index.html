Hello

Well hello there, welcome to the garden. Everything you see here started as a seed. Have a look around, and mind the drip lines.

  • Move: W A S D or the arrow keys.
  • Look: move the mouse. Click the scene once to capture the cursor. Press Esc to release it.
  • Move & look: use the left joystick to walk and the right joystick to look around.
  • Autopilot: the garden shows you around on its own from the moment you step in. Move to take back the controls, and press T or the tour button (top-left) whenever you would like the tour to resume.
  • Settings: the gear icon (top-right) adjusts movement and look sensitivity.
  • Seed to Seed: the logo button (top-right) opens the company's own website in a new tab, so you keep your place in the garden.
