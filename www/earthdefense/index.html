Holding station

Take your time. Nothing moves while this is open.

  • Throttle: W and S, or the up and down arrows. X to stop.
  • Look: move the mouse, or steer with A D to turn and Q E to climb and dive.
  • Throttle: the slider on the left. Hold it up to gather speed and pull it down to shed speed. Let go and it springs back to the middle, and the ship keeps the speed it has. Double tap it to come to a stop.
  • Look: drag the joystick on the right.
  • Guns: there is no fire button. Line a target up in the middle of your view and the guns take care of the rest. The reticle closes into a diamond the moment they have something.
  • The objective: seven installations on Earth and the Moon, and twelve Martian raiders on their way in. Hold the installations and see off the raiders. Both counts are top left, and both only go down.
  • Finding things: every raider carries a diamond, and the closest one is drawn larger with the range to it underneath. Earth and the Moon carry a ring with their distance, and an arrow at the edge of the screen points the way to anything off it.
  • Pausing: Esc, or the pause button in the top right. Nothing moves while this panel is open, and everything you can adjust is right here.

Flight settings

Reduced effects lowers the rendering detail, and it starts switched on for phones and tablets. Turn it off any time you would rather have the fuller sky. Nothing in the game is told by sound alone, so muting loses you nothing.
