Flight Settings

Reduced effects lowers the rendering detail. Handy if the view feels less than smooth on your device. Nothing in the game is told by sound alone, so muting loses you nothing.

Holding station

Take your time. Nothing moves while this is open.

  • Throttle: W and S, or the up and down arrows. X to stop.
  • Look: move the mouse, or steer with A D to turn and Q E to climb and dive.
  • Throttle: the slider on the left. It stays where you leave it, so you can let go. Double tap it to stop.
  • Look: drag the joystick on the right.
  • Guns: there is no fire button. Line a target up in the middle of your view and the guns take care of the rest. The reticle closes into a diamond the moment they have something.
  • The objective: seven installations on Earth and the Moon, and twelve Martian raiders on their way in. Hold the installations and see off the raiders. Both counts are top left, and both only go down.
  • Finding things: every raider carries a diamond, Earth and the Moon carry a ring with the distance to it, and an arrow at the edge of the screen points the way to anything off it.
  • Settings: the gear icon adjusts look sensitivity.

Built in honor of the first generation of video game designers and developers, who worked out with almost nothing that a screen could be a place you go. Left unnamed on purpose. The debt is a shared one.
