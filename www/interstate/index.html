Welcome, need a hand?

Welcome to Interstate Tire. Here's how to look around.

  • Move: W A S D or the arrow keys.
  • Look: move the mouse. Click the scene once to capture the cursor. Press Esc to release it.
  • Move & look: use the left joystick to walk and the right joystick to look around.
  • Autopilot: the shop shows you around on its own from the moment you step in, all the way out to the old freight depot. Move to take back the controls, and press T or the tour button (top-left) whenever you would like the tour to resume.
  • Settings: the gear icon (top-right) adjusts movement and look sensitivity.
  • Heading out? The Home button (top-right) takes you back to the main site, and the logo button beside it opens Interstate Tire's own website in a new tab.
