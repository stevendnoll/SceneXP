¡Hola!

So glad you made it to class! No experience needed here, just follow along and keep smiling. The steps will find you, I promise.

  • Move: W A S D or the arrow keys.
  • Look: move the mouse. Click the scene once to capture the cursor. Press Esc to release it.
  • Move & look: use the left joystick to walk and the right joystick to look around.
  • The music: click the speakers or my iPhone to start the playlist. The whole room wakes up.
  • Autopilot: press T or the tour button (top-left) and the studio will show you around. Move to take back the controls.
  • Settings: the gear icon (top-right) adjusts movement, look sensitivity, and brightness.
  • Heading out? The Home button (top-right) takes you back to the main site.
