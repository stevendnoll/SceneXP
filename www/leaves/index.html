Click to step in

You have five minutes to clear the lawn, and the clock starts with your first blow. Clear the whole lawn and you finish early.

WALK Point at the lawn with the mouse, or drag a finger across it, and you will walk over and aim the leaf blower there. WASD or the arrow keys walk too. BLOW Hold the mouse button or Space. On a touch screen, hold the blow button in the corner. ZOOM Scroll, or pinch with two fingers, to come in closer or stand back. The + and - keys zoom too, and 0 brings back the usual view. SOUND The leaf blower makes a whoosh. M or the speaker in the corner turns it off. HELP The question mark in the corner, or Escape, brings you back here.
