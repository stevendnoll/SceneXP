All the controls
WALK Point at the lawn with the mouse and you will walk over and aim the leaf blower there. WASD or the arrow keys walk too. BLOW Hold the mouse button or Space. ZOOM Scroll, or pinch on a trackpad, to come in closer or stand back. The + and - keys zoom too, and 0 brings back the usual view. SOUND The leaf blower makes a whoosh. M or the speaker in the corner turns it on and off. HELP The question mark in the corner, or Escape, brings you back here.
WALK Touch the lawn and you will walk over and aim the leaf blower there, or drag to steer. The joystick in the lower left walks you too, and a light push turns you in place to aim. BLOW Hold the blow button in the lower right. ZOOM Pinch with two fingers to come in closer or stand back. SOUND The leaf blower makes a whoosh. It starts off on a phone or tablet, so tap the speaker in the corner to hear it. HELP The question mark in the corner brings you back here.
